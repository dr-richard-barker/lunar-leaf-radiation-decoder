Overview & Central Provocation

"Perhaps it's just a classification exercise?"

Bioregenerative life-support systems (BLSS) for the lunar surface (Artemis III Lunar LEAF) depend on fast-cycling crops that can tolerate Galactic Cosmic Rays (GCR) and sustain cellular homeostasis. While machine learning decoders have flourished in Arabidopsis, testing whether these models transfer to crops under real cosmic radiation remained unaddressed.

The Dual-Layer Reality

At the diagnostic layer, YES: The DeepSpace Stress-Recognition System (DSRS) and Arabidopsis ROS Decoder classify 40 cGy GCR as an acute space radiation insult (ρ = 0.255) and predict elapsed kinetics (3.84 h, capturing the crisis nadir).
At the biological layer, NO: It uncovers the Antioxidant Shield Deficit—loss of anthocyanin protection triggers unmitigated superoxide collapse and blunts DNA damage repair, while certifying Deep Space Preservative (DRS) for flight operations.

01 Conceptual Architecture & Diagnostic Pipeline

Bridging Lunar LEAF crop transcriptomics with continuous latent space models.

Figure 1. Hierarchical cross-species radiation decoding framework. The pipeline ingests 39 libraries from Brassica rapa (40 cGy GCR vs 0 cGy, Wild-Type vs anthocyaninless, Deep Space Preservative DRS vs RNAlater), translates loci through the 18,770 1:1 DIAMOND orthology crosswalk, and queries the four-tier decoder stack: DSRS stress recognition, CVAE redox profiling, Radiation Resilience Index (RRI) trajectory projection, and DNA damage repair / fixative parity analysis.

02 DSRS DeepSpace Stress Recognition Single-Cell Fingerprinting

Validating that crop GCR radiation signatures map directly to spaceflight reference models.

We projected the orthology-translated B. rapa signatures onto the 123 single-cell developmental seed panels of the DeepSpace Stress-Recognition System (DSRS). Evaluating the radiation × genotype interaction contrast identified spaceflight and ionizing radiation as the top-ranking reference stressors.

Figure 2. Cross-species stress recognition across 27 reference spaceflight stressors. (A) Top reference stressors matching the B. rapa radiation × genotype interaction signature, led by acute gamma radiation (`rad_100Gy_co60`, ρ = 0.2545) and galactic cosmic rays (`gcr_80cGy`, ρ = 0.2170). (B) Stressor family concordance heatmap: `radiation_GCR` (mean ρ = 0.1504) and `radiation_lowdose` (ρ = 0.1321) consistently dominate over hypergravity, hypoxia, and osmotic stress.

03 Redox CVAE Decoding & The "Antioxidant Shield Deficit"

Disentangling reactive oxygen species cascades and elapsed response kinetics.

Using the 41-dimensional Developmental Stage conditioned Conditional Variational Autoencoder (CVAE) trained on 4,332 GEO transcriptomes, we decoded the chemical nature and temporal stage of the oxidative insult.

Figure 3. Arabidopsis ROS Decoder resolves antioxidant failure in anthocyaninless Fast Plants. (A) Decoded reactive oxygen species probabilities: Wild-Type GCR response is characterized by buffered H2O2 accumulation (51.1%), whereas the anthocyaninless (`anl`) mutant exhibits an unbuffered primary superoxide free-radical burst (Paraquat class 24.9%). (B) Inferred signaling kinetics: Wild-Type GCR corresponds to 3.84 hours post-exposure (Early Acute / Crisis Nadir), while anl exhibits an accelerated acute shock (2.23 hours).

04 Radiation Resilience Index (RRI) & Housekeeping Collapse

Quantifying tissue stability degradation and constitutive stress in mutant backgrounds.

The Radiation Resilience Index (RRI) integrates latent manifold distance (RRIlatent, weight 0.50), pathway activation balance (RRIpathway, weight 0.25), and sustained housekeeping module preservation (RRImodule, weight 0.25).

Figure 4. Radiation Resilience Index (RRI) dynamics in Brassica rapa. (A) Wild-type Fast Plants exhibit an RRI drop from 0.762 in sham controls to 0.636 under 40 cGy GCR (P = 4.2 × 10-4), aligning with the 2–6 hour crisis nadir established in Arabidopsis. Strikingly, anthocyaninless plants at baseline (0 cGy) are constitutively depressed at RRI = 0.637. (B) Housekeeping module preservation collapses from r = 0.481 in WT controls to 0.126 in irradiated plants, and is severely degraded in anl mutants (r = 0.079).

05 Spaceflight Fixative Parity & DNA Damage Repair Core

Evaluating Deep Space Preservative (DRS) vs RNAlater and activation of core repair networks.

Figure 5. Spaceflight fixative parity and DNA damage repair activation. (A) Preservative fidelity comparison: Deep Space Preservative (DRS) demonstrates near-identical RRI profiles and low variance compared to RNAlater (RL), confirming DRS as a flight-certified stabilization reagent. (B) Induction of core DNA double-strand-break repair genes (BRCA1, PARP2, RAD51) and the flavonoid gateway enzymes (PAL1, CHS, F3H) in wild-type plants (+0.61 to +2.03 log2FC), compared to the blunted response in anthocyaninless mutants.

06 Multi-View Spatial Anatomical Susceptibility (ggPlantMap)

Resolving tissue layer vulnerability across vegetative and floral reproductive organs.

Figure 6. Spatial susceptibility heatmap across 11 anatomical tissue layers. Root apical meristem (13.8%) and central vascular stele (12.5%) exhibit the highest vulnerability shares, while floral reproductive organs (anthers, gynoecium, petals) account for > 25% of predicted stress vulnerability.
